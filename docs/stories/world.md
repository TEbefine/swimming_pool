# The world of swimming_pool — core

Read this before any story work. Everything else hangs from it.

## Two layers (Teera, 2026-11-04)
1. **Lumen Bay = the real world.** Players come here to talk, chat and meet NPCs, and to get updates from the real world (real time of day, AI news, daily tips). No game save: the town remembers through community memory.
2. **Other worlds = the story worlds.** They open through the Creator. Each world is its own story and saves on its own.
   - **In the story worlds, the main character is the player (the user).**
   - **Dalbit is only one of these worlds**, and it isn't finished. In Dalbit the player plays as Yunseul; Yunseul is the hero of that one story, not of the whole game.

## The Creator = the Spirit (Teera, 2026-11-04)
- **The Spirit NPC and the Legend card's Creator are the same being.**
- The Creator is like Teera himself: **a bridge between Teera and the other worlds inside him.**
- Special, like a god of this world — **but not a god.** Just a creator you can talk with.
- **Role in the app: the forecaster** (daily fortune / luck). Fortunes are framed honestly: your life is in your own hands.
- The Legend card "The Creator" (1 of 1) is this being. Its line: "Every dreamer needs a way in."

## Rule for every new NPC
NPCs are created step by step. Each new NPC belongs to one layer: Lumen Bay (real world) or one named story world.

## The Creator's pronoun
**It.** (Teera, 2026-11-04)
