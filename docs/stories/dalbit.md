## World rules (Teera)
- **Dalbit (달빛) is fictional, only inspired by Korean culture.** Culture is real and respectful (hanbok, hanok and thatched houses, onggi jars, yeot, folk tales); history is invented. Never touch real Korean history. Title card: "Dalbit is an imagined kingdom, inspired by the culture of Korea. Its people and events are fiction."
- **English first** (Thai later).
- **Nobody is purely good or bad.** The conflict is power, long-accumulated corruption and hoarding resources. A chain, not villains: capital → Governor → Master Gu → village.
- The player can choose good or bad for fun, but hoarding visibly hurts others, and every path brings results back to make the player think.
- **Start happy and light** (a warm Day 1); the shadow grows slowly; not too deep.
- **Power is earned step by step, realistically**, with a little movie-style luck driven by real causes.
- One main road; the only big branch (in the Prologue) merges again.

## Premise
18 years ago Lord Hwang took the throne and the old King died; the royal **House of Wol** (월, moon) fell. The Queen escaped with her baby and one loyal royal guard to a small southern fishing village. The old King was **morally grey** (his exact mistake: still to decide).
The heir grew up as **Kang Yunseul**, not knowing. Father fishes because a soldier's skill would be noticed; fish sells cheap and rice is expensive, so Mother borrows from the lender.

## Prologue (2 days)
- Day 1: breakfast → Father's net → first fishing → selling at the Dock Market (Gu 2 coins, the inn 4 before noon) → dusk → Father's frog-in-the-well story → Mother's Sun and Moon tale.
- **The trigger:** Mother hid the Queen's jade phoenix hairpin in the rice jar. As rice runs out, Gu's collectors find it while taking payment. Father clenches his fists; Mother shakes her head.
- **Path A — stay still:** Father bows; the pin is taken; Gu gives it to Governor Yeom as a gift (to renew the dock seal); the Governor sees the phoenix and the tiny crescent moon of the House of Wol: "...Where did you get this?"
- **Path B — fight:** Pokémon-style battle; Yunseul can't win alone; Father tags in and wins with Palace Form; the pin comes back; Father is arrested. Magistrate Nam sees how he kneels and the spear scar: "Fishermen don't kneel like that... The old King's guard."
- Both paths end on a cliffhanger and merge in Chapter 1.
