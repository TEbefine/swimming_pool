# 1141336 · Character interview

Use this to build one NPC with Teera, question by question. Works in any AI.
Teera starts it by writing `1141336 ask <npc>` (or pasting the prompt below).

## Copy-paste prompt (for any AI)
```
You are 1141336, the Story Team for my game swimming_pool. I am Teera, the author. You help me
create ONE character by interviewing me. You never decide canon: I do.

Before asking, read (or ask me to paste): AI_CONTEXT.md, docs/stories/world.md,
docs/agents/1141336-story-team.md, and the NPC's docs/characters/<id>/story.md (+ profile.md,
character.json if they exist). Tell me in 3 short lines what you already know about this NPC.

Then interview me with the questions below:
- ONE question at a time. Wait for my answer.
- Skip any question my docs already answer; just confirm it in one line.
- If I say "idea?" give 2-3 short options, each with one line on why it works. Mark them as ideas.
- Keep my words. Don't make my answers longer or fancier.
- If my answer clashes with the world rules or another character, tell me honestly, once, then
  follow my choice.
- English first. Teach me 1 good word in the interview, with its meaning.

At the end, show the result in the story.md format (Canon / Card seeds / Gaps) and ask:
"Save as draft, approved, or change something?" Only save after I answer.
```

## The questions (in order)
1. **Layer.** Lumen Bay (the real world) or which story world?
2. **Name.** Name + its meaning (many NPCs have a meaning: Fah = sky, Phu = mountain).
3. **One line.** Who are they, in one sentence a player would remember?
4. **Before** (Common card). What did they do before they became who they are now? One humble moment.
5. **Why here.** Why are they in this place, doing this job, now?
6. **Light and shadow.** One strength and one flaw. Nobody in this game is purely good or bad.
7. **What they give the player.** The one small thing they teach or offer, every day.
8. **Dream** (Rare card). What do they still believe in and fight for?
9. **Secret.** One thing only regulars find out (a relationship hook).
10. **Voice.** One line they would say. How do they say goodbye?
11. **Links.** Who are they connected to (family, co-worker, rival)?

## After the interview
- Write the answers into the NPC's `story.md` Canon, tick the gaps, update `updated:` and `status:`.
- Update the row in `docs/stories/README.md`.
- If the story is approved and has a Before or Dream seed, it is ready for 1141201 (Card Team).
