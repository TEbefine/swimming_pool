# Pan (ปัน) — story

**Role:** Host of the Weekday Chill Café and founder of SOIBAO. Pronouns: she/her.

## Canon (LOCKED by Teera, 2026-11-04, worked out with Gemini)
- **One line:** A former city office worker who came home to Lumen Bay to brew SOIBAO coffee that draws out the exact feeling each customer needs.
- **Name:** Pan (ปัน, "to share").
- **Before:** She worked in a busy city office, then came back to her hometown, Lumen Bay. She brewed her first cup at a tiny street stall because she understood how exhausted office workers feel.
- **Why here, now:** She found a quiet spot in Lumen Bay to live simply, be free, and understand her customers' feelings through her coffee.
- **Light:** A deep passion for improving SOIBAO and matching every cup to a customer's emotion, to make them truly happy.
- **Shadow:** She puts too much pressure on herself to perfect SOIBAO and her menu, and forgets to look back at herself, her family and the people closest to her.
- **What she gives the player:** Soft, calm energy, one useful tip a day, and ordering coffee from her menu triggers a once-a-day relaxation scene.
- **Dream:** "Unlimited Coffee BAO on Top of the Sky" — a cup so light and pure that drinking it lifts you up to float freely in the sky.
- **Secret and the player's part:** Her menu is made for only ONE person at a time, never a group, because she worked so hard she forgot her family and the people around her. The player helps her see it, and her solo dream turns into her true realization: **coffee for everyone, together.**
- **Voice:** "Take a breath — let this cup make your day a little lighter." Ends every visit with a warm wai.
- **Links:** Teacher and mentor of Sun (`server`), her student who is learning the whole coffee process from her.
- Unchanged from earlier canon: Thai, late 20s; teaches one small thing a day (สอนแล้วเบาขึ้น); calm listener who notices how you feel.
