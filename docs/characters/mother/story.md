# Mother — the hidden Queen — story

**Role:** Village housekeeper; secretly the Queen of the House of Wol

## Canon
Only what Teera's docs already say. Lines marked (draft) were proposals in the source doc.

- Late thirties. Fled with her baby 18 years ago. Gentle, warm, a little tired, with an upright grace she can't fully hide. Nothing royal on her.
- Wears a plain wooden binyeo; her real jade phoenix hairpin is hidden in the rice jar.
- Borrowed from the lender to buy rice. "Half full. It's always half full."
- Breaks the yeot in halves: sweet things taste better in halves.
- Tells the folk tale of the siblings who became the Sun and the Moon (foreshadowing).
- When Gu's collectors find the pin, she shakes her head at Father: the Queen shows through for one moment.
