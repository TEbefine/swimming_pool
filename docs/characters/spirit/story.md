# The Creator (the Spirit) — story

**Role:** Temple keeper; reads daily omens; the door to other worlds

## Canon
Only what Teera's docs already say. Lines marked (draft) were proposals in the source doc.

- **Pronoun: it** (Teera, 2026-11-04). Never "she" or "he".
- **NEW (Teera, 2026-11-04): the Spirit and the Creator are the same being.** Like Teera himself: a bridge between Teera and the other worlds inside him. Special, like a god of this world, but NOT a god — just a creator to talk with. Role in the app: the forecaster. See docs/stories/world.md.
- It is the door to other worlds. You can enter any time, each story saves on its own, and inside a story you find it again to come home.
- Small role: daily luck / fortune. Big role: carries the player's spirit into many stories (from history to the future, even a magic Wonderland).
- Omens can be good or bad, framed honestly as a traditional art: your life is in your own hands.
- Faceless: a plain white hooded cloak, a black void, one thin pale arm. Mysterious and ขลัง. No extra fantasy (no gold, stars, halo, staff, lantern).
- Home: a pure-white, quiet, Parthenon-like temple with big columns.
- In Dalbit it keeps the Ledger of Echoes: small choices that come back later.
